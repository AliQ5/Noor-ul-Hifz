The project

Built with care for real institute workflows.

Noor-ul-Hifz was created to replace scattered daily sheets with a consistent, practical record system. The goal isn't to make administration complicated. It's to make the information already being collected easier to keep, review and use.

نور الحفظ means "Light of Memorisation."
