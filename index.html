A focused workflow

From a daily Excel sheet to an organized record in seconds.

Noor-ul-Hifz is designed around the way institutes already work. Import the familiar daily report, review the records, choose the correct date and save. Your history stays local and searchable.

Simple on the surface.

Flexible Dates

How it works

Four calm steps. No clutter.

01

Select

Choose the daily Excel report from your computer.

02

Review

See the file name, student count and category totals before saving.

03

Set date

Use today's date or select a previous date for historical records.

04

Save

Store the day locally and use it later for reports and totals.

✓
Local by design

Your institute's records stay with your institute.

The application is built around local storage. Your daily records don't need a remote dashboard, subscription or always-on internet connection.

The project

Built with care for real institute workflows.

Noor-ul-Hifz was created to replace scattered daily sheets with a consistent, practical record system. The goal is not to make administration complicated — it is to make the information already being collected easier to preserve, review and use.
